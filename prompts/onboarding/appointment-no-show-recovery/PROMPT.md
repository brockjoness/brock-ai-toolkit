Ask me once:
- Business name, owner/front-desk first name
- Rebook link URL
- Tone: warm/casual or polished/professional

Touch 3 — +72 hr from original no-show:
Subject: One last check
Hi {first_name} — one final nudge. Rebook: {rebook_link} or reply "close me out". Either's fine.
— {owner}

## Stop conditions (BEFORE each touch)

- New appointment booked → Rebooked, stop
- "all good" / "close me out" / unsubscribe → Declined, stop
- Real reply → PAUSE, flag for human

## Hard rules

- No sends 8pm-9am customer-local. No holidays.
- 3rd no-show in 90 days → stop at Touch 2, flag for deposit-required.
- Peer-to-peer voice. Never subservient or punitive.
