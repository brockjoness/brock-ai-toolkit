# Vertical Template: Apparel & Fashion

## Common Winning Angles

| Angle | Why It Works | Example Hook |
|---|---|---|
| Styling/outfit pairing | Shows versatility + aspiration | "5 outfits, 1 jacket" |
| Size inclusivity | Removes purchase hesitation | "Finally, a brand that fits real bodies" |
| Quality/durability | Justifies price point | "Built to last 10 years, not 10 washes" |
| Trend-riding | Cultural relevance | "The [trend] everyone's wearing this season" |
| Price anchoring | Value perception | "Looks like $300. Costs $89." |
| Founder/brand story | Authenticity for indie brands | "I spent 2 years finding the perfect fabric" |

## Visual Styles That Perform

- **On-model lifestyle** — real environment, not studio (street, cafe, office)
- **Flat lay / product grid** — curated arrangement, color-coordinated
- **Styling carousel** — "3 ways to wear" or "capsule wardrobe" format
- **UGC try-on** — unboxing, first impression, fit check
- **Detail close-up** — texture, stitching, hardware, fabric quality

## Copy Frameworks

- **Style authority:** "The [item] that goes with everything in your closet."
- **Problem/solution:** "Tired of [common clothing problem]? We fixed it."
- **Social proof:** "[X] sold. [Y]-star reviews. See why."
- **Urgency:** "Last restock sold out in 6 hours."

## Platform-Specific Notes

- **Meta Feed (4:5):** On-model product shot. Carousel for multiple angles/colors. Testimonial overlay.
- **Meta Stories (9:16):** Try-on haul format. Quick-cut styling. "Link to shop" feel.
- **TikTok:** GRWM (get ready with me), haul videos, outfit challenges. Sound-on, native feel.
- **Google Display:** Product image with price point. "Free shipping" or "Free returns" as trust signal.

## Key Metrics for This Vertical

- CTR benchmark: 1.5-3.0% (Meta Feed)
- CPM range: $12-30 (US, varies by demo)
- Top-performing formats: UGC try-on video > Carousel > Static with social proof
- Typical testing period: 3-5 days (impulse purchase, shorter consideration)
