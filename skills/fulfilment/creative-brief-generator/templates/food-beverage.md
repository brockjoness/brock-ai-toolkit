# Vertical Template: Food & Beverage

## Common Winning Angles

| Angle | Why It Works | Example Hook |
|---|---|---|
| Taste/flavor experience | Sensory desire | "You've never tasted coffee like this" |
| Health benefit callout | Functional food trend | "More protein than a chicken breast" |
| Origin/sourcing story | Quality + authenticity | "Single-origin, hand-roasted, shipped same day" |
| Convenience/ease | Removes friction | "Gourmet dinner in 10 minutes" |
| Subscription value | LTV play | "Never run out again. Cancel anytime." |
| Comparison/switch | Competitive displacement | "We taste-tested 20 brands. This won." |

## Visual Styles That Perform

- **Food photography** — warm lighting, close-up, steam/texture visible
- **Lifestyle context** — product in morning routine, kitchen, dinner table
- **Ingredient showcase** — raw ingredients surrounding finished product
- **Unboxing/subscription reveal** — package arrival, "what's inside" format
- **Recipe/preparation** — quick-cut prep video, satisfying process shots

## Copy Frameworks

- **Sensory-first:** "[Taste/texture/aroma description]. That's what [product] delivers."
- **Health benefit:** "[X] grams of [nutrient]. Zero [bad thing]. All [good thing]."
- **Origin story:** "Grown in [place]. Roasted in [place]. In your cup by [timeline]."
- **Social proof:** "[X] 5-star reviews. [Y] cups shipped. Try the one everyone's talking about."

## Platform-Specific Notes

- **Meta Feed (4:5):** Close-up food photography. Recipe carousel. Subscription offer card.
- **Meta Stories (9:16):** Quick recipe / preparation video. "Morning routine" format. Taste test reaction.
- **TikTok:** Recipe content, taste test reactions, "foods I can't stop eating" format. Sound-on.
- **Google Display:** Product image with price. "Free shipping on first order" or subscription discount.

## Key Metrics for This Vertical

- CTR benchmark: 1.5-2.5% (Meta Feed)
- CPM range: $10-25 (US, varies by product)
- Top-performing formats: Recipe video > Static with offer > UGC taste test
- Typical testing period: 3-5 days (consumable, quick repurchase cycle)
