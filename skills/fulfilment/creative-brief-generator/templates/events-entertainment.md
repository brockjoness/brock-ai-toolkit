# Vertical Template: Events & Entertainment

## Common Winning Angles

| Angle | Why It Works | Example Hook |
|---|---|---|
| FOMO/scarcity | Time-bound urgency | "Only 200 tickets left" |
| Lineup/talent showcase | Star power drives interest | "You won't see this lineup anywhere else" |
| Experience preview | Aspiration + anticipation | "This is what last year looked like" |
| Early bird pricing | Price anchoring + urgency | "Save $50 if you buy before Friday" |
| Social proof / crowd | Bandwagon effect | "12,000 people. One night. Don't miss it." |
| Behind-the-scenes | Exclusivity + authenticity | "Here's what we're planning for this year" |

## Visual Styles That Perform

- **Past event highlight reel** — crowd energy, stage shots, atmosphere
- **Lineup graphic** — artist/speaker names, dates, clear CTA
- **Countdown format** — urgency-driven with date overlay
- **UGC from attendees** — real people at past events, testimonials
- **Venue/location showcase** — beautiful setting, experience tease

## Copy Frameworks

- **Urgency:** "[X] days left. [Y] tickets remaining. Lock yours in."
- **Experience-first:** "Imagine [experience description]. That's [event name]."
- **Social proof:** "[X] people came last year. [Y] are already signed up for this one."
- **Value stack:** "[Headliner] + [Headliner] + [experience] + [perk]. One ticket."

## Platform-Specific Notes

- **Meta Feed (4:5):** Past event photo/video with lineup overlay. Ticket price callout. Countdown.
- **Meta Stories (9:16):** Highlight reel from past events. Countdown sticker. "Get tickets" swipe-up.
- **TikTok:** BTS content, attendee reactions, lineup reveals. Sound-on with event music.
- **Google Display:** Event name + date + price. Location callout. "Buy tickets" CTA.

## Key Metrics for This Vertical

- CTR benchmark: 2.0-4.0% (Meta Feed — events drive high engagement)
- CPM range: $8-20 (geo-targeted, event-interest audiences)
- Top-performing formats: Video highlight reel > Carousel lineup > Static with countdown
- Typical testing period: 2-3 days (time-sensitive, fast decision cycle)
