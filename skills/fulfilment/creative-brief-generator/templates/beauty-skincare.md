# Vertical Template: Beauty & Skincare

## Common Winning Angles

| Angle | Why It Works | Example Hook |
|---|---|---|
| Before/After transformation | Visual proof of results | "What 30 days actually looks like" |
| Ingredient callout | Educated buyer cares about what's inside | "The one ingredient your dermatologist won't shut up about" |
| Routine simplification | Skincare fatigue is real | "Replace 5 products with 1" |
| Skin concern specific | Pain-point targeting by condition | "If your skin does this, read carefully" |
| Real user testimonial | Social proof from real people | "I almost didn't believe my own skin" |
| Founder story | Authenticity + trust | "I made this because nothing else worked for me" |

## Visual Styles That Perform

- **UGC-style selfie** — genuine, bathroom mirror, natural lighting
- **Clinical/clean aesthetic** — white background, product + ingredients, medical authority
- **Before/after split** — side-by-side or swipe (most engaging format for skincare)
- **Texture close-up** — product on skin, sensory experience
- **Flat lay** — product surrounded by ingredients or lifestyle elements

## Copy Frameworks

- **Problem → Solution → Proof:** "Tired of [problem]? [Product] does [benefit]. [Social proof stat]."
- **Ingredient-led:** "[Ingredient] does [specific thing]. Here's why your skin needs it."
- **Routine disruption:** "Stop using [common product]. Use this instead."
- **Transformation timeline:** "Day 1 vs. Day 30. No filter."

## Platform-Specific Notes

- **Meta Feed (4:5):** Before/after works best. Product shot with text overlay. Testimonial quotes.
- **Meta Stories (9:16):** Tutorial-style or "get ready with me" feel. Swipe-up for offer.
- **TikTok:** Sound-on UGC. "Dermatologist reacts" or routine videos. Native, not polished.
- **Google Display:** Ingredient callout with clean product shot. Benefit-led headline.

## Key Metrics for This Vertical

- CTR benchmark: 1.5-2.5% (Meta Feed)
- CPM range: $15-35 (US, women 25-45)
- Top-performing formats: UGC video > Static before/after > Carousel
- Typical testing period: 5-7 days (skincare has longer consideration)
