# Vertical Template: Health & Wellness

## Common Winning Angles

| Angle | Why It Works | Example Hook |
|---|---|---|
| Science/research backed | Trust through authority | "Backed by 47 clinical studies" |
| Daily habit integration | Low-friction adoption | "Add this to your morning coffee" |
| Symptom-specific targeting | Pain point precision | "If you wake up tired, your body is telling you something" |
| Results comparison | Social proof + specificity | "I tried 12 supplements. This is the only one I kept." |
| Doctor/expert endorsement | Authority credibility | "Why 3,000 doctors recommend this" |
| Clean label / transparency | Ingredient-conscious buyer | "3 ingredients. Zero filler. That's it." |

## Visual Styles That Perform

- **Lifestyle integration** — product in daily context (kitchen counter, gym bag, desk)
- **Clean/minimal product shot** — white or earth-tone background, ingredient callouts
- **Side-by-side comparison** — this product vs. competitors (transparency play)
- **Expert authority** — lab coat, medical setting, clinical aesthetic
- **User testimonial video** — real person describing their experience

## Copy Frameworks

- **Symptom → Cause → Solution:** "Still [symptom]? It's not [common assumption]. It's [root cause]. [Product] fixes that."
- **Authority-led:** "[Expert type] says [insight]. That's why we made [product]."
- **Clean label:** "[X] ingredients. [Y] benefits. Zero BS."
- **Habit stacking:** "You already [existing habit]. Just add this."

## Platform-Specific Notes

- **Meta Feed (4:5):** Science-backed claims with product shot. Testimonial quotes with real names.
- **Meta Stories (9:16):** "Day in my life" format. Quick benefit highlight. Doctor talking head.
- **TikTok:** Sound-on expert content. "Things I wish I knew" format. Ingredient education.
- **Google Display:** Benefit-led headline with trust badge. "Clinically proven" or "Doctor recommended."

## Key Metrics for This Vertical

- CTR benchmark: 1.2-2.0% (Meta Feed)
- CPM range: $20-40 (US, health-conscious adults)
- Top-performing formats: Expert video > Static with testimonial > UGC unboxing
- Typical testing period: 5-7 days (supplement/health has trust barrier)
