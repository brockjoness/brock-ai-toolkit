# SKILL 2: Creative Analysis

## When to use

After Skill 1 has cataloged all active ads and mapped messaging angles.

## What to do

### Subsection 1: Creative Patterns

Identify the 3 strongest creative patterns across the prospect's active ads. Judge by observable signals:
- Recency (newer = likely working)
- Variant count (more variants of an angle = the brand is scaling it)
- Production quality and copy strength

For each pattern:
- **Pattern name** (descriptive, e.g., "Emotional gifting angle with unboxing format")
- **Evidence**: Which ads demonstrate this pattern? How many? How long have they been running?
- **Why it works**: Explain the psychology -- what emotional or rational trigger does this pattern hit? Why does it stop the scroll?
- **Replication guidance**: How could this pattern be adapted or expanded?

### Subsection 2: Format Mix Assessment

Evaluate the overall creative balance:

| Format | # of Ads | % of Total | Assessment |
|---|---|---|---|
| Video | X | X% | [Over/under/balanced] |
| Static | X | X% | [Over/under/balanced] |
| Carousel | X | X% | [Over/under/balanced] |
| UGC | X | X% | [Over/under/balanced] |

Flag concentration risks: Is the brand over-reliant on one format? Are proven formats (in the industry) completely absent?

### Subsection 3: Copy & Messaging Mining

Extract actual headlines, hooks, and value props from the prospect's strongest-looking ads. Present as a usable reference library.

**Working Headlines** (from ads that appear to be performing based on recency/variant signals):
- "[Exact headline]" -- Ad #X -- Why it works: [one sentence]
- Group by type: Emotional/Aspirational, Functional/Benefit-Driven, Urgency/Promo, Identity-Based

**Hook Types Observed**:
- What types of opening lines/hooks is the brand using?
- Which hook types appear in their longest-running ads (likely winners)?
- Which hook types appear only in recently launched or short-lived ads (likely tests or misses)?

**Value Prop Patterns**:
- What specific claims, benefits, or differentiators does the brand emphasize?
- Are they leading with emotional or rational messaging? Both?
- What's missing? (e.g., no social proof, no urgency, no founder story)

**CTA Analysis**:
- All "Shop Now" or a healthy mix?
- Opportunities: test benefit-driven CTAs ("See the Collection", "Find Your Size", etc.)

### Subsection 4: Video Creative Notes (Conditional)

Only if video ads are present in the Ad Library.

Analyze observable elements (no retention data available):
- **Hook types**: What appears in the first 3 seconds? (text overlay, face-to-camera, product shot, action sequence)
- **Apparent pacing**: Fast cuts or slow pans? How dynamic is the editing?
- **Format style**: UGC vs. polished brand content vs. hybrid
- **Text overlay usage**: Present or absent? Emotional or functional?
- **Sound-off readiness**: Does the ad rely on audio, or do text overlays carry the message?

Note: "Full video analysis (retention rates, drop-off points, hook effectiveness by data) requires account access -- available in a post-access audit."

### Subsection 5: Static Creative Notes (Conditional)

Only if static ads are present in the Ad Library.

Analyze:
- **Design patterns**: What visual compositions are being used? (flat lay, on-model, graphic composite, lifestyle)
- **Copy integration**: How is text placed on the image? (top-third, center, bottom CTA)
- **Color palette**: Consistent with brand, or inconsistent?
- **Call-out strategies**: Size labeling, fabric callouts, bundle savings, badges?

## Output format

1. **Top 3 Creative Patterns** (with why it works and replication guidance)
2. **Format Mix Table** (with assessment)
3. **Copy & Messaging Library** (headlines, hooks, value props, CTAs)
4. **Video Creative Notes** (if applicable)
5. **Static Creative Notes** (if applicable)
