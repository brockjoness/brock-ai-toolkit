# SKILL 5: Opportunities & Next Steps

## When to use

After all prior skills have run. This is always the final analysis skill in the pre-access audit.

## What to do

### Subsection 1: Top 3 Things Working

From the creative analysis (Skill 2), identify the 3 strongest elements in the prospect's current creative strategy.

For each:
- **What**: Name the specific pattern, angle, or tactic
- **Evidence**: Which ads demonstrate this? How many? How long running?
- **Why it works**: Explain the psychology -- what emotional or rational trigger does it hit?
- **Recommendation**: How to scale or expand this strength

Frame positively. This shows the prospect you understand their business and validates what they're already doing well.

### Subsection 2: Top 3 Things to Stop or Fix

From the creative analysis and website review, identify the 3 biggest creative weaknesses or missed opportunities.

For each:
- **What's happening**: Name the specific issue
- **Why it fails**: Explain what's going wrong and the likely impact
- **Do instead**: Provide a concrete, specific alternative -- not vague advice
- Reference specific ads or pages where the issue appears

Frame as fixable opportunities, not failures.

### Subsection 3: Creative Testing Roadmap

Based on all findings, propose specific creative tests. Each entry needs:

**Video Concepts to Test** (2-3):
- Concept name
- Format (length, aspect ratio, style)
- Hook / opening line
- Structure (shot-by-shot or beat-by-beat)
- Why test (data or gap justification)
- Recommended landing page

**Static Concepts to Test** (2-3):
- Concept name
- Visual direction
- Copy / headline
- Why test
- Recommended landing page

**Copy Angles to Test** (2-3):
- Current approach vs. proposed test
- Why test (what gap does this fill?)

Keep each concept description to 3-5 sentences. This is a roadmap, not full briefs.

### Subsection 4: What We Would Do With Full Access

THIS IS THE SALES HOOK. 3-5 bullets on what a full post-access audit would reveal that the pre-access cannot:

Examples:
- "With ad account access, we would identify your actual top-performing ads by ROAS and double down on proven winners -- not just what looks strong from the outside."
- "We would run a fatigue analysis to catch ads that are losing steam before they waste budget."
- "We would map your audience segments to find which demographics are driving the most revenue and which are burning spend."
- "We would analyze your full conversion funnel -- from click to purchase -- to find exactly where prospects are dropping off."
- "We would benchmark your CPMs, CTRs, and ROAS against your historical data to set realistic performance targets."

Adapt bullets to the prospect's specific situation (e.g., if they have many video ads, mention video retention analysis; if they're an ecommerce brand, mention landing page conversion data).

## Output format

1. **Top 3 Things Working** (with why and how to expand)
2. **Top 3 Things to Stop or Fix** (with why and what to do instead)
3. **Creative Testing Roadmap** (video concepts, static concepts, copy angles)
4. **What a Full Audit Would Reveal** (3-5 bullets -- the sales close)
