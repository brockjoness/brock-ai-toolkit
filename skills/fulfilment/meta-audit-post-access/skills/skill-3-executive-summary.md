# SKILL 3: Executive Summary

## When to use

After baselines are established.

## What to do

### Step 1: Account Snapshot Table

Open the Executive Summary with a snapshot table:

| Metric | Value |
|---|---|
| Report Date | [date] |
| Total Ads Analyzed | X |
| Total Spend | $X |
| Total Revenue (Meta attributed) | $X |
| Weighted ROAS | X.XXx |
| Total Orders | X |
| Best Creative ROAS | X.XX (creative name) |
| Worst Creative ROAS | X.XX (creative name) |

### Step 2: Top 3 Creative Winners

Identify the 3 highest-performing ads by ROAS (with sufficient spend/sample). For each:

**[Ad Name]**
- **Spend:** $X | **ROAS:** X.XXx | **Orders:** X | **New Customer %:** X%
- **Why it works:** 3-5 sentences explaining the creative psychology. What emotional or rational trigger does this ad hit? What format/hook/copy choices drive the performance? What makes this ad work for its audience? Reference specific visual and copy elements. Connect performance metrics to creative decisions (e.g., "85% play rate with 25% watching to 25% mark = strong hook. The emotional text overlay creates immediate context before showing product.").

### Step 3: Top 3 Creative Patterns to Replicate

Abstract patterns from the winners -- not specific ads, but repeatable approaches:

For each pattern:
- **Pattern name** (descriptive, e.g., "Emotional gifting angle with unboxing format")
- **Why:** Which ads demonstrate this? What evidence shows it works? Cite specific metrics.
- **Replicate:** Specific tactical instructions for how to scale this pattern (e.g., "Shoot 5 new videos in this format across [product categories]. Use [hook type]. Target [demographic].")

### Step 4: Top 3 Things to Stop Doing

Identify the 3 biggest creative failures or wasteful patterns:

For each:
- **What's failing:** Name the specific issue (e.g., "Generic 'Just Launched' messaging without emotional hooks")
- **Why it fails:** Which ads demonstrate this? What evidence shows it's not working? Cite specific metrics and explain the psychology of why it doesn't convert.
- **Do instead:** Concrete, specific alternative with examples (e.g., "Every new product launch needs an emotional or functional hook. Examples: 'Mom of a baseball kid? Same.' or 'The only St. Patrick's outfit you actually want them to wear all day.'")

### Step 5: Key Learnings Summary

Write a concise summary covering:
- **Seasonal Context:** What time period does the data cover? What seasonal factors are relevant?
- **Creative Formats:** Which formats outperform? Which underperform? Key format insights.
- **Audience Signals:** What audience patterns emerge from the data?
- **Messaging Themes:** What messaging approaches work? What doesn't?

## Output format

1. Account Snapshot table
2. Top 3 Creative Winners (with detailed "why it works")
3. Top 3 Creative Patterns to Replicate (with tactical replication instructions)
4. Top 3 Things to Stop Doing (with concrete alternatives)
5. Key Learnings Summary
