# SKILL 4: Opportunities & Next Steps

## When to use

After all prior skills have run. This is always the final analysis skill for pre-access audits.

## What to do

### Subsection 1: Top 3 Things Working

From everything observed across ad copy, landing pages, and competitive position, identify the three strongest elements.

For each:
- **What's working:** One-sentence description
- **Why it works:** 2-3 sentences explaining the strategic reasoning -- connect to search intent, conversion psychology, and competitive differentiation
- **How to expand:** One concrete action to do more of this (new ad variation, new format, new landing page)

### Subsection 2: Top 3 Things to Stop or Fix

Identify three practices or elements that are likely hurting performance.

For each:
- **What to stop/fix:** One-sentence description
- **Why it fails:** 2-3 sentences explaining why this is a problem -- reference observable signals (weak copy, mismatched landing pages, missing formats, no extensions)
- **What to do instead:** A specific alternative -- not vague advice, but a concrete change with example copy or structure

### Subsection 3: Quick-Win Recommendations

5-7 specific, implementable changes that don't require account access to recommend:

1. Ad copy improvements (specific headline or description suggestions)
2. Extension additions (which types to add and what content to include)
3. Landing page fixes (specific changes to improve message match and conversion)
4. Format expansion (which new ad formats to test and why)
5. Messaging angle gaps (which angles to add based on competitor analysis)

### Subsection 4: What a Full Audit Would Reveal

This is the **sales close**. Frame as a natural next step, not a hard sell.

Structure:
> "From the outside, we can see [summary of what the pre-access audit revealed]. But the real leverage is inside the account. With access to your Google Ads Manager, we would also analyze:"

Then list 5-7 things that require account access, with one sentence each explaining the value:

- **Search term waste:** "We'd identify exactly which search terms are burning budget and add the right negative keywords to cut waste"
- **Quality Score optimization:** "We'd find the keywords where low Quality Score is inflating your CPC and prioritize fixes by impact"
- **Keyword-level ROAS:** "We'd see which exact keywords drive revenue and which just drive clicks, so we can reallocate budget to winners"
- **Bidding strategy audit:** "We'd evaluate whether your current bidding strategy is optimal for your goals or leaving money on the table"
- **Shopping/PMax diagnostics:** "We'd assess your product feed quality, PMax search term match quality, and identify which products deserve more or less budget"
- **Budget allocation analysis:** "We'd calculate exactly where you're leaving impression share on the table and where budget is being wasted"
- **Conversion path analysis:** "With GA4 access, we'd map the full customer journey from click to conversion and identify where people drop off"

## Output format

1. **Top 3 Things Working** (with why + how to expand)
2. **Top 3 Things to Stop/Fix** (with why + concrete alternatives)
3. **Quick-Win Recommendations** (5-7 actionable items)
4. **What a Full Audit Would Reveal** (the sales close)
