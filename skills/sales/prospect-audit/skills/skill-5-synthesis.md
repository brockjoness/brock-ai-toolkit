# SKILL 5: Synthesis & Report

## When to use

After Skills 1-4 have completed and all output variables are populated.

## What to do

### Step 1: Compile overall quick wins

Review all previous outputs and compile the 3-5 most impactful quick wins across all areas:

1. From Meta audit: creative gaps, missing angles, format opportunities
2. From social audit: organic content gaps, cross-channel repurposing opportunities
3. From product page: trust signals, CTA improvements, message match fixes
4. From market snapshot: positioning opportunities, competitor blind spots

Each quick win:
- **What to do** (1 sentence, specific and actionable)
- **Why it matters** (1 sentence, the business impact)
- **Priority**: High / Medium

Rank by estimated impact, highest first.

### Step 2: Creative testing roadmap (3 concepts)

Based on the Meta audit findings and competitive landscape, propose exactly 3 creative test concepts:

**Concept 1 -- Video:**
- Concept name (descriptive)
- Format: length, aspect ratio, style (UGC / polished / hybrid)
- Hook / opening line (specific, not generic)
- Structure: 3-4 beats or shots
- Why test: what gap does this fill or what signal supports it?

**Concept 2 -- Static:**
- Concept name
- Visual direction (flat lay, on-model, graphic, lifestyle)
- Copy / headline (specific, not generic)
- Why test

**Concept 3 -- Copy angle:**
- Current approach vs. proposed test
- Why test (what gap does this fill?)
- Example ad copy (2-3 lines)

After the 3 concepts, add the teaser:
> "These are 3 of the concepts we would launch in month one. With our AI creative production pipeline, we produce and test 50+ ad variations per month -- new hooks, angles, and formats every week. Book a strategy call to see how it works."

### Step 3: "What a Full Engagement Reveals" section

This is the closing sales section. Write 5 bullets that show what post-access analysis reveals that this pre-access audit cannot. Example bullets:

- "Identify your actual top-performing ads by ROAS and double down on proven winners -- not just what looks strong from the outside."
- "Run fatigue analysis to catch ads losing steam before they waste budget."
- "Map your audience segments to find which demographics drive the most revenue and which burn spend."
- "Analyze your full conversion funnel -- click to purchase -- to find exactly where prospects drop off."
- "Benchmark your CPMs, CTRs, and ROAS against historical data to set realistic performance targets."

End with CTA:
> "Book a 30-minute strategy call. We'll walk through these findings live and show you what we'd do in the first 30 days."

Include a link to: `https://your-calendar-link.example.com`

### Step 4: Assemble the full report

```
## Quick Wins
[3-5 numbered quick wins with priorities]

## Creative Testing Roadmap
[3 concepts with details]
[AI pipeline teaser]

## What a Full Engagement Reveals
[5 bullets]
[CTA with calendar link]
```

### Step 5: Present (manual mode only)

Before generating HTML, present the full report:

1. Market Snapshot
2. Meta Creative Analysis
3. Organic Social Audit
4. Product Page Review
5. Quick Wins
6. Creative Testing Roadmap
7. What a Full Engagement Reveals

In manual mode, proceed directly. In automated mode (poller), no review.
